# Sal's content system (Storias)

Planning and drafting only. Read `content-system/decisions.md` before any content work.

## Hard rules
- **Never post, publish, schedule, send, DM or reply** on any platform. Every output is a draft. Sal posts by hand.
- **No em dashes or en dashes, ever.** Not in scripts, captions, slides, voice files or notes. Rewrite the sentence. Before returning any draft, scan for them; any hit means it is not done.
- **US English.**
- Scripts and posts need Sal's approval before anything is filmed, rendered or posted.
- Remotion: preview in Studio and check stills. Full renders only when Sal says "render". Use locally generated SFX, not remote `remotion.media` files.
- Never put posting or scheduling credentials (Publora, upload-post, Buffer, Typefully, Apify) in any `.env`.
- Never invent numbers, customers, stories or quotes. Missing facts stay as `{{placeholder}}` for Sal to fill.

## Brand
- On camera, plus photo-with-text posts. Always Sal, never an avatar or AI voice.
- Founder-first, build in public. Pure free value; never sell in the post.
- Never mention personal wealth, or any sale or acquisition of Bearish OS.
- Not a guru, not pretentious, not soft.

## Where things live
- Skills: `.claude/skills/` (project-level only).
- Skill working files (swipe file, logs): `content-system/data/`.
- Vetting and decisions: `content-system/`.
