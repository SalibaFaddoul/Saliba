# Voice interview questions (asked Oct 8, 2026)

Sal answers in his own words, ideally by voice-to-text. Answers go in `content-system/voice/answers.md`, verbatim. Do not clean them up beyond filler ("um", false starts).

## Warm up
1. What did you eat today, and was it a good call?
2. What's something you changed your mind about in the last year?
3. What's an opinion you hold that most people your age would argue with you about?
4. What's the most overrated thing in tech right now?
5. What's the most underrated?

## Building
6. Tell me the story of the first 10 paying customers like you're telling a friend at dinner.
7. What was the moment you thought "oh, this might actually work"?
8. What's the dumbest mistake you've made building Storias?
9. What does a normal Tuesday look like for you, hour by hour, honestly?
10. When you're stuck, what do you actually do?
11. What do people get wrong about "building with AI"?
12. If Storias died tomorrow, what would you build next?
13. What does "real work vs performed work" mean to you? Give an example of each from this week.

## Money, work, attention
14. What did your parents teach you about money, on purpose or by accident?
15. What's something you'll never spend money on? Something you'll always spend on?
16. What does being "busy" mean to you, and do you respect it?
17. Where does your attention leak? What steals it?
18. What's a piece of startup advice you think is flat out wrong?

## You
19. Who taught you how to think? A person, a book, anything.
20. What did you want to be when you were 10?
21. What's something you're weirdly good at that has nothing to do with work?
22. What makes you laugh every time?
23. What makes you genuinely angry?
24. What's a small thing you're proud of that nobody would ever notice?
25. Where are you happiest?

## Generation
26. What does your generation get right that older people miss?
27. What does your generation get wrong?
28. If you could say one thing to every 22-year-old, what is it?
29. What's a word or phrase you say all the time? Friends would know.
30. How would your best friend describe you in one sentence? Your harshest critic?

## Random
31. Last thing you Googled that you'd be embarrassed to admit?
32. A hill you'll die on that doesn't matter at all?
33. What's a song you've had on repeat?
34. If you had to give a 60-second talk to strangers right now, no prep, what would it be about?
35. Finish this sentence ten different ways: "Nobody wants to hear this, but..."
